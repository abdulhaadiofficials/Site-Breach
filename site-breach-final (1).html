Pick a deck and a threat level. You drop at the map edge with a pistol, a knife and two frags. The deck is held.

Reach SITE A or SITE B and hold E for three seconds to arm the breach charge. The deck wakes up. Later decks twist the contract — hunt a high-value target, spike both sites, or hold out under a live alarm.

HOLD

Survive the counter-attack until detonation. Every hostile down is score — the rating is yours to keep.
